Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common Minecraft hosting questions.

Can players from US/Europe join my server?

Yes, absolutely! Our network infrastructure is optimized to provide low-latency routing worldwide, ensuring a seamless experience for International players connecting to your hosting server.

What is included in HexaCraft's Minecraft Hosting?

All plans include NVMe storage, unmetered bandwidth, full FTP access, free MySQL databases, top-tier DDoS protection, and a custom Pterodactyl-based control panel.

Can I host a Minecraft server for free?

No, we do not offer free hosting. However, our premium plans are extremely affordable, guaranteed to offer the utmost performance with stable resources.

How much RAM do I need for my server?

It depends on your version and plugins. We recommend at least 4GB for vanilla servers with a few friends, and 8GB+ for heavy modpacks or large player counts.

Is HexaCraft the best Minecraft host in India?

Yes! Our servers run on premium Indian infrastructure in Tier-4 datacenters yielding ultra-low latency (<20ms) for unmatched performance.

How do I install mods or modpacks?

You can use our integrated 1-click Modpack Installer available directly from your personalized game panel, covering thousands of popular modpacks.

Can I upload my own custom modpack or JAR?

Absolutely. You get unrestricted access to your files via SFTP or the built-in web file manager, allowing any custom JARs or personal modpacks.

Do you support versions like Forge, Fabric, Paper, or Purpur?

Yes, we support all major server forks and versions! You can swap between Paper, Purpur, Spigot, Forge, and Fabric anytime through our version changer.

Is the Minecraft server always online?

Yes. Our systems are backed by a 99.99% uptime guarantee, meaning your server stays resilient, persistent, and 24/7 accessible to your community.

Can I upgrade my Minecraft server later?

Of course! Upgrades are instantaneous. You can scale up your RAM, storage, or CPU power seamlessly without losing any of your world data or setup.

Do you provide free subdomains?

Yes! Every standard purchase includes completely free, customizable subdomains (e.g. yourname.hexacraft.net) so you don't need to purchase a dedicated IP or domain.

How do I access my Minecraft panel?

After checkout, credentials will be immediately delivered to your billing email granting you instant access to our interactive management portal.

Is there a server limit for players?

No! All our game server plans completely uncap your player slots. You dictate the capacity based exclusively on the RAM allocated from your chosen plan.

Do you offer backups?

Yes. We offer fully automated off-site backups available directly natively integrated into your game panel securing your world data against griefers.

Can I host a Bedrock server?

Absolutely! You can choose Bedrock Edition out of the box or utilize plugins like GeyserMC on a Java server so cross-play users can join effortlessly.
